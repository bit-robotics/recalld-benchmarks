# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this repository to be a place where anyone can challenge a benchmark
result without it becoming personal, including people who work on systems we
measure, and who may disagree with how we measured them.

## Expected behaviour

- Argue about methodology, configuration and numbers.
- Assume a bad result is a mistake before assuming it is bad faith.
- Accept a correction when the evidence supports it, and say so publicly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately misrepresenting another project's performance or configuration.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **support@recalld.ai** with "Code of Conduct" in the subject
line. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit, hide or delete
contributions that breach this document, and may block repeat offenders.
